# Story: Investigating dynamics of covid-19 spread and containment with agent-based modeling

## Research Motivation

This repository includes an epidemic agent-based model that simulates the spread of Covid-19 epidemic. Normal.nlogo is the main file, while Exploring-zoning.nlogo and Exploring-Testing-With-Tracking.nlogo are modefied models to test the two strategies and run experiments.

## Research Goal

Replicate and extend the Investigating dynamics of covid-19 spread and containment with agent-based modeling model. Analyze emergent dynamics through parameter exploration and sensitivity analysis.
